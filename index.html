Drip Rides — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026
Contact: evanser194@gmail.com — Eric Evans, individual developer

The short version

Drip Rides exists to show off builds, not to harvest data. We collect what the app needs to work — your account, the builds and posts you share, and the inputs you give the AI features — and nothing else. We don't run ads, we don't sell your information, and we don't track you across other apps.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address when you create an account. Passwords are handled by Google's Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password.
  • Profile: display name, profile photo, and the build styles/makes you pick during onboarding (used to personalize your feed).
  • Your content: build photos and videos, posts, comments, likes, ratings, contest entries (including the entry video), your garage cars and cost-estimator entries, and marketplace listings.
  • AI features: questions you ask the AI Build Advisor, and the car photos plus mod descriptions you submit for AI previews. These are sent to the AI services below to generate your result.
  • Feedback: anything you send through the in-app feedback button.

Information collected automatically

Very little. The app currently ships no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs. Firebase keeps the standard operational logs any hosted app needs (e.g., authentication events). App settings are stored on your own phone, not on our servers.

Payments

At launch, Pro subscriptions and contest entry fees are processed by Google Play. We never see, touch, or store your card details — that's between you and Google.

How we use it

To run the app: your builds, rankings, contests, and garage. To answer you: the AI advisor and previews. To keep the family safe: enforcing the zero-tolerance policy on rudeness, harassment, and hate. To reach you: only about the app itself.

Who else touches your data

We don't sell your information to anyone. It is processed by the services that power the app, each under its own terms:

  • Google Firebase — account login, database, and backend hosting (data stored in the US)
  • Google Gemini — AI Build Advisor chat and parts search
  • Replicate — AI mod previews of your car
  • Cloudinary — photo hosting
  • Google Play — subscriptions and contest payments

Builders under 16

Posting builds has no age limit — a kid who built with family can share their ride. A parent or guardian may manage the account of a builder under 16 and can ask us to delete it at any time. Paid competitions require entrants to be 16 or older, and a guardian must receive any winnings.

Your control

Email evanser194@gmail.com any time to see, correct, or delete your information and content. Deleting your account removes your profile and content from our servers; routine backups expire on their normal cycle.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and access-controlled backend services. No method is 100% secure, and no app can promise that.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with an updated date. Material changes get an in-app notice.
